In 2011 Chad Harcrow and Tom Winters bought the Adobe Rose B&B. With Chef Chloe Winters, they developed a family-run restaurant in the historic adobe building, blending New Mexico traditions with newer culinary ideas and emphasizing local farms, New Mexico food and drink, and investment in the Artesia community.
